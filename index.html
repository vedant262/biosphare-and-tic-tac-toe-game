1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Dragoster Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Tic Tac Toe Modern mobile game application ("the App") available on Google Play.

We do not run our own servers, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal details ourselves. The App does, however, display advertisements through Google AdMob to keep the game free — and like almost every free app with ads, that means our advertising partner (Google) automatically collects some device-level data to show and measure those ads. This page explains exactly what that involves.

🛡️ Short version: We personally collect nothing about you. The game itself has no accounts, no logins, and stores your progress only on your own device. The only data leaving your device is what Google AdMob automatically collects to serve ads, described in Section 3 below.

2 Information We (Dragoster Studios) Do Not Collect

Beyond what our advertising partner collects (see Section 3), we ourselves do not collect, request, or store any of the following:

✕ Personal information ✕ Email addresses ✕ Names or usernames ✕ Precise location data ✕ Crash or analytics reports ✕ Purchase history ✕ Contact information ✕ Photos, camera, or microphone access
3 Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) so that the game can remain free to play. To do this, the AdMob SDK running inside the App automatically collects and shares certain data with Google, including:

Advertising ID (AAID) & App Set ID IP address (approximate location) Device & app information Ad interaction data (views/taps)

This data is used by Google to display and personalize ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraudulent activity. All of it is transmitted securely (encrypted in transit).

Your controls: You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID"). This does not require contacting us and takes effect immediately.

To learn more about how Google collects and uses this data, see: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and Google's Partner Privacy Policy.

4 Internet & Permissions

The App requires an internet connection to load and display ads. Gameplay itself does not need a network connection, but the app will request internet access on install because of the ad SDK.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or precise location. The only permissions present are the ones Android and Google AdMob automatically add for serving ads (such as internet access and the advertising ID permission).

5 Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into this App is Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), used solely to display advertising. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, or any other tracking or analytics platform.

✓ Google AdMob (ads only) ✕ No Google Analytics ✕ No Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics ✕ No Facebook SDK

You can review Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

6 Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. However, because the App now shows ads through Google AdMob, parents and guardians should be aware that Google's standard (non-child-directed) advertising data practices described in Section 3 apply, unless the app's Google Play "Target audience" declaration specifies otherwise.

If this App is ever positioned as being made for children, additional AdMob settings (such as child-directed treatment / limited ads) and Google Play Families Policy requirements would need to be configured separately from this general policy.

7 Data Security

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your own device and are never transmitted to us. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising-related data described in Section 3, which is handled by Google under its own security practices (encrypted in transit via TLS).

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, when app features change, as they did when ads were added. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
